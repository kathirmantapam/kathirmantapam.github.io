# Privacy Policy for Kathirmandapam

**Effective Date:** October 4, 2026

Welcome to Kathirmandapam ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our matrimonial profile listing application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access or use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect
To provide a reliable matrimonial listing service, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register, create a profile, or interact with the App.

* **Personal Profile Details:** Full name, age, gender, date of birth, marital status, education, occupation, family background, religious/community preferences, and other bio-data details required for matrimonial matching.
* **Contact Information:** Phone numbers and email addresses so prospective matches can contact the listed profiles.
* **Profile Photos:** Images and photographs uploaded for the matrimonial profiles.
* **Device & Usage Data:** Basic technical data such as IP address, device type, operating system, and app usage logs to ensure app security and performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

* **To Provide and Maintain the Service:** Creating and managing user profiles for prospective brides and grooms.
* **To Facilitate Communication:** Displaying contact numbers and profile details to registered users to help them connect for matrimonial purposes.
* **To Improve the App:** Monitoring app performance, debugging issues, and enhancing user experience.
* **To Ensure Safety and Compliance:** Preventing fraudulent profiles, verifying user authenticity, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
We employ robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:

* **Database Storage:** User profile text data and structured information are stored securely using cloud database infrastructure (currently Supabase, subject to change in future migrations).
* **Media Storage:** Profile photos and visual media are stored securely in cloud object storage spaces backed by Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* **Security Practices:** We utilize encryption in transit (HTTPS/SSL) and secure access controls to guard against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your data.

## 4. How We Share Your Information
Kathirmandapam is a matrimonial listing platform, which inherently involves sharing certain details with other users:

* **Public Profile Visibility:** Profile details, bio-data attributes, and contact numbers uploaded to the App will be visible to other registered users of Kathirmandapam for the purpose of matrimonial matchmaking.
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of our users or others.
* **Service Providers:** We may share data with trusted cloud hosting and database providers (such as Supabase, AWS, and GCP) strictly for operating and maintaining the App. These providers are bound by strict data protection standards.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices
* **Access and Update:** You can view, edit, or update your profile details and photos directly within the App at any time.
* **Account Deletion:** You have the right to request the deletion of your profile and associated data from our database and cloud storage by contacting us directly. Once deleted, your profile and contact numbers will no longer be visible on the App.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Kathirmandapam is strictly intended for individuals who are of legal age to marry (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we discover that a user is under 18, we will take immediate steps to delete their profile.

We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
